Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025  ·  Effective date: July 2025

PJ Antique Bali (operated by CV Karya Bersama, "we", "us", or "our") operates the website at antique.id. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

This website is a display-only showroom. We do not sell products online, do not process payments, and do not create user accounts.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information only when you voluntarily submit it through our contact form. This includes:

  • Name — as provided by you
  • Email address — to respond to your enquiry
  • Phone number — optional, for WhatsApp or call follow-up
  • Message content — the enquiry or request you submit
  • Category of interest — the product category you select

We do not collect information automatically via cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools at this time. Standard server access logs (IP address, browser type, page requested, timestamp) may be recorded by our hosting provider (Cloudflare) for security and operational purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry about our products or showroom
  • Arrange a showroom visit, video call, or product discussion
  • Follow up on your interest in a specific product or commission

We do not use your information to:

  • Send unsolicited marketing emails or newsletters
  • Sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Build advertising profiles

3. Contact Form Service Provider

Our contact form is processed by Formspree (formspree.io). When you submit the contact form, your data is transmitted to and temporarily stored by Formspree in order to forward your message to us. Please refer to Formspree's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle form submission data.

4. Data Retention

We retain your contact information only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and conduct any related business discussion. Email records are retained in accordance with standard business practice. If you would like your information removed from our records, please contact us at info@antique.id.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information from our records

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at info@antique.id. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Third-Party Links

Our website contains links to third-party websites and services, including Google Maps, Instagram, and WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites. Please review their respective privacy policies.

7. Security

Our website is served over HTTPS. Contact form submissions are encrypted in transit. While we take reasonable precautions, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Your continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
