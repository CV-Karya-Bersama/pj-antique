Our Craft

Ancient Earth, Timeless Beauty

Millions of years ago, trees fell in Indonesia's volcanic forests. Over time, mineral-rich groundwater replaced every cell of organic matter with crystalline stone — creating petrified wood.

At Petrified Wood Indonesia, we find these geological treasures and give them new life as extraordinary furniture and décor. Each piece is unique, carrying within it the record of ancient earth.
